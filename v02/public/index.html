Four Pillars.
Shaping the Future of Work.

01
Train AI Workers
Developing comprehensive training programs to equip workers with cutting-edge AI skills—from prompting and data concepts to building, fine-tuning, and maintaining AI-powered solutions.
02
Support Small Businesses & Nonprofits
Bringing AI-powered solutions to local businesses and nonprofits to boost productivity, enhance services, and create new opportunities for underserved communities.
03
Pilot Programs & Trials
Partnering with employers on real-world initiatives to demonstrate how AI augments jobs, protects workers, and drives measurable impact in frontline roles.
04
Achieve Systemic Changes
Working with policymakers to develop responsible AI deployment frameworks that protect workers while enabling progress.
